Snag packages yt-dlp and FFmpeg as separate command-line programs.

yt-dlp
  Project: https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp
  License: The Unlicense
  Source and releases: https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/releases

FFmpeg
  Project: https://ffmpeg.org/
  License: LGPL 2.1 or later; particular distributed builds may enable GPL components
  Source: https://ffmpeg.org/download.html#get-sources

Run `yt-dlp --version` and `ffmpeg -version` to inspect the exact bundled builds.
